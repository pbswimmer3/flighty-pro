# Flight Dash Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-11

Flight Dash is a flight tracker for iPhone. It has no account, no analytics and no server of its own. The developer does not collect, receive or sell any data about you.

## What stays on your device

- The flights you add, your Passport stats and delay history.
- Your settings, favourite airports and alert choices.
- Your AeroDataBox API key, if you enter one. It is stored on this device in the app's preferences.

None of this is sent to the developer. To delete it, use Settings, then Remove All Flights (and Clear Punctuality History), or delete the app.

## Who the app talks to

Flight Dash contacts these services directly from your device. They see your IP address and the request itself, under their own privacy policies.

- FAA National Airspace System Status (nasstatus.faa.gov): airport delay status.
- NOAA Aviation Weather Center (aviationweather.gov): airport weather (METAR), sent an airport code.
- adsb.lol: live aircraft positions, sent a flight callsign or an area around an airport.
- adsbdb.com: route lookup, sent a flight callsign.
- Apple Maps (MapKit): the map tiles, under Apple's privacy policy.
- AeroDataBox via RapidAPI: only if you enter your own key and turn Demo Mode off. Flight numbers and dates you look up are sent, along with your key.

In Demo Mode, flights are generated on the device and no flight lookup is made.

## Notifications

Flight alerts are local notifications scheduled on your device. There is no push server.

## Location, tracking and children

The app does not ask for your location, contacts, photos or camera, and does not track you across other apps or sites. It is not directed at children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date above will change with it.
